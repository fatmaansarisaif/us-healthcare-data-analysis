# us-healthcare-data-analysis
This project analyzes US healthcare data to identify patterns in patient demographics, healthcare costs, insurance spending, and hospital resource utilization.  The objective was to generate actionable insights that can help healthcare organizations improve patient outcomes, optimize costs, and efficiently allocate resources.

**Tools Used: Microsoft Excel**:
Data Cleaning
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Slicers
Dashboard Design
Business Insights
Data Visualization
Presentation Storytelling

**Key Business Insights**
Cancer patients spend approximately 70% more than the average patient.
Diabetes patients spend approximately 28% more than average.
Insurance providers such as Aetna and Cigna are associated with higher healthcare spending.
Certain medications may help reduce treatment costs for specific conditions.
Admission trends can help hospitals optimize resource allocation and staffing.

**Business Objectives**:
1. Demographic Analysis
Identify the most prevalent medical conditions across demographic groups.
**Questions answered:**
Which medical condition is most common?
Which age groups have the highest patient volume?
Which medical conditions are prevalent by age?
How do conditions differ by gender?
What trends exist across blood groups?

2**. Patient Cost Optimization**:
Analyze healthcare expenses and identify opportunities to reduce costs.
**Questions answered:**
Which admission types incur higher costs?
Which medical conditions are more expensive?
Which medications contribute to higher billing?
How does insurance provider impact healthcare spending?

3.** Hospital Resource Management**:
Evaluate admission trends to improve hospital operations.
**Questions answered:**
How do admissions vary over time?
Which periods experience higher workloads?
How can hospitals optimize staffing and resources?

**Key Insights**

**Healthcare Costs**
Cancer patients spend approximately 70% more than average patients.
Diabetes patients spend approximately 28% more than average patients.
Insurance Insights
Patients insured through Aetna and Cigna incur higher spending.
Blue Cross patients exhibit lower average healthcare costs.

**Medication Insights**
Cancer patients on Lipitor treatment experience lower costs.
Asthma patients treated with Lipitor also demonstrate reduced expenses.
Operational Insights
Admission trends can help hospitals anticipate resource demand and improve workforce planning.

**Recommendations**
Develop targeted interventions for high-cost patient groups.
Negotiate treatment plans with insurance providers.
Monitor medication effectiveness to reduce unnecessary spending.
Forecast admission patterns to optimize staffing.
